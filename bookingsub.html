Questions

Is $150 a one-time payment or monthly?
One payment of $150 covers a full year of unlimited access. No hidden fees, no automatic charges — you renew manually when you're ready.
What happens when my subscription expires?
Your account stays active but booking is locked. You can still browse artists and manage existing requests — you just can't send new ones until you renew.
Can I renew early?
Yes. If you renew while still active, the new 12 months are added on top of your existing expiry — you never lose days.
How long does approval take?
Typically within 2 hours during the day, and always within 24 hours. You'll be notified in your inbox the moment it's approved.
What if my payment is rejected?
You'll receive a notification with the reason. You can generate a new invoice and try again, or contact support via WhatsApp for help.
I forgot my password — can I reset it?
Yes. Open the Sign In window and tap “Forgot password?”, then enter your email. A secure reset link will be sent to you — be sure to check your inbox and your spam / junk folder.
