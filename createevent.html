1. Platform Fee: Zone21 charges a 2% fee on every ticket sold through the platform. This fee is automatically deducted from the ticket price before payout to the organizer.

2. Event Approval Workflow: All events are submitted as Pending Approval. An administrator will review and either approve or reject the event. Approved events are live and visible to the public with ticket purchasing enabled.

3. Organizer Responsibilities: The organizer is fully responsible for all event logistics, compliance with local laws, venue arrangements, and any required permits or licenses.

4. Image Rights: All uploaded images (posters, ticket designs, etc.) must be owned by the organizer or properly licensed for use.

5. Event Cancellation: Organizers may request cancellation of an approved event. Cancellation requests require admin approval and may be subject to fees.

6. Payment Processing: Zone21 processes payments via its payment partners; organizers will receive net proceeds after deduction of the 2% platform fee and any applicable payment processing fees.

7. Prohibited Content: Events promoting illegal activities, hate speech, or violence are strictly prohibited and will be rejected.
