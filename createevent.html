Zone21 Organizer Terms & Conditions

2% ZONE21 TICKETING COMMISSION
For every ticket sold through the Zone21 platform, the Organizer agrees that Zone21 will receive a 2% ticketing commission. The commission is calculated on the ticket price paid by the customer for each ticket sold through Zone21, unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise.

Example

If a customer purchases a ticket for US$100:

  • Ticket price: US$100
  • Zone21 commission (2%): US$2
  • Amount remaining before any other applicable deductions: US$98

If 1,000 tickets are sold at US$100 each:

  • Total ticket sales: US$100,000
  • Zone21 2% commission: US$2,000
  • Remaining amount: US$98,000, before any other applicable deductions.

The 2% Zone21 commission applies to each ticket successfully sold through the Zone21 platform, subject to the terms of the applicable payment and settlement arrangement.

Ticket Sales and Settlement

Where Zone21 receives ticket payments on behalf of the Organizer, Zone21 may deduct its 2% ticketing commission before transferring the applicable balance to the Organizer.

Zone21 may also deduct or withhold other amounts where legally and contractually permitted, including:

  • Valid refunds;
  • Chargebacks;
  • Reversed or fraudulent transactions;
  • Payment-processing charges where applicable;
  • Other amounts expressly agreed between Zone21 and the Organizer.

The Organizer will receive the remaining balance according to the agreed settlement schedule.

Off‑Platform Ticket Sales

The Organizer must not deliberately direct customers away from Zone21 for the purpose of avoiding the agreed 2% Zone21 ticketing commission where the customer or ticket sale originated through Zone21. Where an Event is marketed, listed or ticketed through Zone21, the Organizer agrees to cooperate with Zone21 in maintaining accurate ticket-sales records. The Organizer must not manipulate, conceal or misrepresent ticket sales for the purpose of reducing Zone21’s contractual commission.

Ticket Refunds and the 2% Commission

Where a ticket is refunded, the treatment of the Zone21 2% commission will depend on the reason for the refund and the applicable refund arrangement. Zone21 may reverse or adjust its commission where appropriate. Where the Organizer is responsible for a refund, the Organizer remains responsible for providing the funds necessary to satisfy the refund where applicable. Zone21 is not required to use its own funds to cover refunds arising from the Organizer’s cancellation, postponement, failure to provide the Event or other circumstances for which the Organizer is responsible, except where Zone21 is legally required to do so.
