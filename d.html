Our Story

Zone21 Events was established in 2025 by its founder, Mr. Dean Mwanza, with a simple idea: event planning should not be this complicated.

The idea came from observing the challenges faced by people organizing events. Finding reliable service providers often meant travelling from place to place, spending valuable time searching, comparing prices, and hoping that the person or company found through social media would actually deliver what they promised.

At the same time, many event organizers have a specific budget but struggle to understand how to allocate it effectively. They may know how much they want to spend, but not how much should go towards a venue, security, entertainment, catering, production, photography, transportation, or other essential services.

Zone21 Events was created to bring all of this together.

Our Mission

To simplify event planning by connecting organizers with reliable service providers, helping them make smarter decisions with the resources they have available — all through one unified digital platform.

Our Vision

To build one of the world's leading digital event ecosystems — starting from Zimbabwe and expanding beyond borders — where clients can discover trusted partners, plan events, manage budgets, sell tickets, and coordinate teams without searching in dozens of different places.

What We Do

Four Pillars of Zone21

01

Connect Organizers with Trusted Providers

Venues, security, entertainment, catering, production, photography, transport and more — all in one place. No more searching across social media or travelling from place to place.

We help clients develop budgets and event blueprints, so they know exactly how to allocate funds across every essential service — from venue hire to security to entertainment.

03

Ticketing & Digital Event Management

Our platform makes it easy to purchase event tickets online, while organizers gain reliable tools to sell and manage tickets seamlessly — all part of the Zone21 ecosystem.

04

Through Zone21 Security we provide trained personnel for events and other security needs. Our vision includes an on-the-ground team capable of preparing, coordinating and managing events — even for clients outside the country.
